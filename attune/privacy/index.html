Attune privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026. Applies to Attune for Mac and Attune for iPhone.

The short version

Attune has no servers and Cranky Walrus collects nothing about you through the app. Your recordings, transcripts, notes and voice library are stored on your own devices. Data only leaves your devices when you choose a cloud service for transcription or AI, and then only goes to that service. Confidential meetings never leave your Mac. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.

Who is responsible

Attune is made by Cranky Walrus (crankywalrus.dev). For anything about this policy or your data, write to privacy@crankywalrus.dev.

What Attune stores, and where

  • On your Mac (in ~/Library/Application Support/Attune/): meeting recordings, transcripts, minutes and other outputs, slides captured during meetings, voice notes, the people you name (name, company, pronouns), the voice library, your settings, a log of AI token usage, and your licence file.
  • Voiceprints: to recognise who is speaking, Attune keeps a numerical voiceprint of each person you name. Voiceprints are biometric data. They are created and kept only on your Mac, never sent to any service, and never used for anything other than labelling speakers in your own transcripts.
  • On your iPhone: recordings until your Mac has received them, and the summaries and drafts your Mac sends back.
  • In your iCloud (iPhone app only): recordings travel from your iPhone to your Mac through your own iCloud Drive, in a private app folder. The Mac removes the audio from iCloud once it has it. Apple stores this under your Apple Account; Cranky Walrus has no access to it.

Permissions Attune asks for

PermissionUsed for

You can withdraw any of these at any time in System Settings (Mac) or Settings (iPhone) › Privacy & Security. Attune keeps working without the optional ones.

What leaves your devices, and to whom

Nothing is sent to a cloud service until you choose that service in Attune's settings. Data is then sent directly from your Mac to that service, over an encrypted connection, only to perform the task you asked for:

Built-in model, Local Whisper or Apple's speech engineNothing: these run entirely on your Mac. —
  • Confidential meetings are always transcribed on your Mac and are never sent to any cloud service.
  • You use these services with your own account (your API key or your subscription). The provider processes the data under your agreement with them. With the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs, the providers state that API data is not used to train their models by default; with a ChatGPT or Claude subscription, the data settings of your own account apply. We only support providers whose terms protect your data at least as well as this policy.
  • You can switch to on-device processing at any time (Settings › General); from then on nothing more is sent.
  • Model downloads: the speech and speaker models, and the optional built-in AI model, are downloaded once from Hugging Face. That is an ordinary file download; none of your content is sent.
  • Minutes, Teams and WhatsApp messages are only copied to your clipboard or saved to folders you choose. Attune does not send messages on your behalf.

What Cranky Walrus collects

Nothing through the app. Attune contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting, does not contact any Cranky Walrus server, and does not share data with data brokers. Token usage shown in Attune is counted on your Mac and stays there. Licences are checked on your Mac, without a network connection.

If you buy a licence from us directly, we receive the name, email address and (for a licence tied to one Mac) the machine code you give us. We use them only to issue your licence and to answer your questions, keep them as long as your licence is valid plus the period tax law requires, and share them with no one. Purchases through the App Store are handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment details.

How long data is kept

  • Everything on your devices stays until you delete it.
  • Recordings in iCloud are removed as soon as your Mac has received them.
  • Cloud services keep what they receive according to their own policies and your account settings (linked above).

Deleting your data

  • Delete meetings and voice notes in Attune; they go to the macOS Bin.
  • Delete voiceprints in Speakers: per sample, per person (Reset voice) or all at once (Reset voice profiles).
  • Remove the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Attune/ to delete everything Attune stored on your Mac. Deleting the iPhone app removes its local data.
  • To have licence details deleted, write to privacy@crankywalrus.dev.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete and receive a copy of personal data about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Since everything Attune creates is on your own devices, you can do most of this in the app yourself. For licence details, contact us.

Recording other people

Meetings include other people's voices, and Attune can learn their voiceprints. You are responsible for telling participants that you record, for getting their consent where required, and for following the law and your organisation's policies where you and they are. Delete a person's voiceprints in Speakers if they ask you to.

Children

Attune is a tool for work meetings and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Changes that affect how your data is handled are also mentioned in the release notes of the app.
