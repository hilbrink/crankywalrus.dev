Attune privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026. Applies to Attune for Mac, Attune for iPhone, the website crankywalrus.dev and support by email.

The short version

Attune has no servers. Your recordings, transcripts, notes and voice library stay on your own devices, and I cannot see them. Data only leaves your devices when you choose a cloud service for transcription or AI, and then it goes directly to that service under your own account. Confidential meetings never leave your Mac. There is no advertising, no tracking, no analytics and no cookies. I only process personal data myself when you visit this website, email me or get a licence from me, as described below.

2. Which law applies

I live in Switzerland and process personal data under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Where I offer Attune to people in the European Union or the European Economic Area, I also comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The terms used in this policy (such as personal data, controller, processing) have the meaning they have in the GDPR.

3. Attune: your data stays on your devices

Everything Attune creates is processed on your Mac and iPhone. I do not receive, store or have access to it. For this content you are the controller, not me.

  • On your Mac (in ~/Library/Application Support/Attune/): meeting recordings, transcripts, minutes and other outputs, slides captured during meetings, voice notes, the people you name (name, company, pronouns), the voice library, your settings, a log of AI token usage, and your licence file.
  • Voiceprints: to recognise who is speaking, Attune keeps a numerical voiceprint of each person you name. Voiceprints are biometric data. They are created and kept only on your Mac, never sent to any service, and only used to label speakers in your own transcripts.
  • On your iPhone: recordings until your Mac has received them, and the summaries and drafts your Mac sends back.
  • In your iCloud (iPhone app only): recordings travel from your iPhone to your Mac through your own iCloud Drive, in a private app folder, under your Apple Account. The Mac removes the audio from iCloud once it has it. I have no access to it.
  • Licences are checked on your Mac, without a network connection. Attune contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting of its own, and it never contacts a server of mine.

Cloud services you choose

Nothing is sent to a cloud service until you choose one in Attune's settings. Data is then sent directly from your Mac to that service, over an encrypted connection, using your own API key or subscription. The provider processes it under your agreement with them; I am not involved and receive nothing.

If you use Attune at work: recording other people

Meetings contain other people's voices and words, and Attune can learn their voiceprints. For this processing you (or your employer) are the controller. Under the GDPR and the FADP that means, among other things: tell participants that you record and transcribe, have a legal basis for it (for voiceprints, which are biometric data, usually their explicit consent), choose a cloud service only if you may share the meeting with it, and delete a person's voiceprints in Speakers when they ask. Follow your organisation's policies and the law where you and they are.

4. What I process myself

4.1 Visiting crankywalrus.dev

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser and operating system, in order to deliver the page and protect the service against abuse. GitHub keeps these logs according to its own privacy statement; I do not receive them.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The site sets no cookies, contains no analytics or tracking, and loads no fonts or scripts from other services.

4.2 Emailing me (support and privacy questions)

When you write to support@crankywalrus.dev or privacy@crankywalrus.dev, I process your email address, name, the content of your message and anything you attach (for example a log file or a screenshot), to answer you and to solve the problem. Please don't send recordings or transcripts unless I ask; remove anything confidential from logs.

Legal basis: our agreement, or steps you asked for before one (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and otherwise my legitimate interest in answering your questions (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention: I delete support correspondence 24 months after the matter is closed, unless I have to keep it longer by law.

4.3 Getting a licence

From me directly: licences are free. I process your name, email address, your company if you give it, the licence details (licence number, period, features) and, for a licence tied to one Mac, the machine code you send me. I use them to issue and renew your licence and to help you with it.

Legal basis: our agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Retention: licence data as long as your licence is valid and for 2 years after it ends.

Through the App Store: Apple distributes the app and handles the download under Apple's privacy policy. I do not receive your name or email address. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Apple device settings, Apple gives me crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

7. How long data is kept

  • Everything Attune stores on your devices stays until you delete it. Recordings in iCloud are removed as soon as your Mac has received them.
  • Support emails and licence data: as stated in section 4.
  • Cloud services you chose keep what they receive according to their own policies and your account settings.

8. Your rights

Under the FADP and the GDPR you have the right to:

  • find out whether I process personal data about you and receive a copy of it (access);
  • have incorrect data corrected and incomplete data completed;
  • have your data deleted, or its use restricted;
  • receive data you gave me in a common, machine-readable format, or have it sent to someone else (portability);
  • object to processing based on my legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent you have given, with effect for the future.

Write to privacy@crankywalrus.dev. I answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity. These rights can be limited by law.

For data Attune keeps on your own devices you can do this yourself: delete meetings and voice notes in Attune (they go to the macOS Bin); delete voiceprints in Speakers per sample, per person (Reset voice) or all at once (Reset voice profiles); remove ~/Library/Application Support/Attune/ to delete everything on your Mac; delete the iPhone app to remove its data.

Complaints: you can complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch), or, if you live in the EU/EEA, to the data protection authority in your country. I'd appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

9. Other information

  • Do you have to give me data? No. Without your email address I can't answer you, and without licence details I can't issue a licence.
  • Automated decisions: I make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing, and I do not profile you.
  • Security: the website and email use encrypted connections. Attune keeps your data on your devices; I recommend FileVault on your Mac and a passcode on your iPhone.
  • Children: Attune is a tool for work meetings and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly process children's data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Changes that affect how your data is handled are also mentioned in the release notes of the app.
